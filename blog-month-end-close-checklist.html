A Practical Month-End Close Checklist for Small Finance Teams

A structured month-end close checklist covering bank reconciliations, ledgers, accruals, supporting documents and review controls.

Published 25 November 2026 · 6 min read

Digicafe · Practical guide

Month-end close is easier to manage when routine checks happen in a fixed order. A checklist helps an accounts team track what is complete, what is pending and which balances need review. The exact process depends on the organisation's accounting policy and reporting requirements.

1. Collect the source records

Gather bank statements, payment gateway reports, sales and purchase registers, expense claims, payroll summaries, loan statements and relevant supporting documents. Confirm that the records cover the full accounting period.

2. Complete bank and settlement reconciliations

Match bank entries with the cash or bank ledger using date, amount and transaction reference such as UTR where available. Separately reconcile payment gateway settlements, fees, refunds and chargebacks. List unmatched items with an owner and follow-up date.

3. Review customer and supplier balances

Check aged receivables and payables, unapplied receipts, advances, credit notes and debit notes. Investigate old or unusual balances instead of carrying them forward without explanation.

4. Check journals and cut-off

Review recurring entries, accruals, prepayments, depreciation and provisions according to the organisation's policy. Confirm that income and expenses are recorded in the correct period and retain supporting calculations and approvals.

5. Review statutory and control accounts

Reconcile relevant GST, TDS, payroll, loan and other control accounts with their supporting registers and returns. Differences should be explained and resolved or documented for follow-up.

6. Prepare a close summary

Record completed tasks, pending items, material variances, journal references and reviewer sign-off. Keep a clear audit trail so another team member can understand each adjustment.

Simple close tracker columns

  • Task and account name
  • Period covered and preparer
  • Amount or balance reviewed
  • Status: Not started, In progress, Completed or On hold
  • Exception, supporting file link and reviewer

Good practice: Do not post an adjustment solely because a spreadsheet flags a difference. Verify the source, accounting treatment and approval first. This checklist is general guidance, not a substitute for your organisation's accounting policy or professional review.
