What is the bouquet effect?

A bouquet-effect visual combines a floral arrangement with photographs, portrait details or memory-inspired elements. AI image tools can help explore this concept in a polished, cinematic style. The result depends on the quality of the reference photos, prompt clarity and the image tool being used.

From an ordinary photo to a digital keepsake

01 · Choose

Select clear, high-resolution photographs. Use images you own or have permission to edit.

02 · Describe

Specify the people, bouquet arrangement, wrapping, colour palette, lighting and overall mood.

03 · Refine

Review facial details, hands, text and image composition. Regenerate or edit areas that look inaccurate.

Example prompt to get started

Create an elegant bouquet-themed portrait using the supplied reference photographs. Arrange the photos naturally among deep red roses and delicate white flowers, wrapped in premium burgundy paper with a satin ribbon. Use warm cinematic lighting, realistic floral textures, refined luxury styling and a clean composition. Preserve the subjects' recognisable facial features from the references. Do not invent extra people, logos or text.

Tip: Add the exact aspect ratio and output size required by your platform. If accurate faces matter, use a tool that supports reference images and inspect the result before sharing.

Which AI tools can help?

  • ChatGPT: develop, structure and refine prompts for different creative directions.
  • Gemini: explore image-generation and editing workflows available in your account.
  • Nano Banana Pro: use supported image-editing workflows for detailed visual concepts and iterations.
