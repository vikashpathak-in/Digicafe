GSTR-2B and Purchase Register Reconciliation: A Practical ITC Review Workflow

Published 27 November 2026 · 7 min read

A clean GSTR-2B review starts with a reliable purchase register, a consistent matching method and a documented exception list. The aim is not to make every line match at any cost. It is to understand each difference before the relevant input tax credit (ITC) is considered in the return.

The GST Portal describes Form GSTR-2B as a static, auto-drafted statement built from information furnished by suppliers and Input Service Distributors. It is a read-only statement, not a return that the recipient files. It helps taxpayers review ITC for GSTR-3B, but it does not replace the recipient's own eligibility checks.

1. Prepare the purchase register first

Before comparing files, close the period's purchase register and make sure each record has the fields needed for matching. At a minimum, retain:

  • Supplier GSTIN and supplier name
  • Invoice or debit-note number, document type and date
  • Taxable value and the IGST, CGST and SGST amounts
  • Place of supply, where relevant
  • Credit notes, debit notes and amendment references
  • Import, Input Service Distributor (ISD) and reverse-charge items identified separately

Keep the original invoice number as received. If you create a cleaned or normalised matching key in Excel, store it in a separate column rather than overwriting the source value.

2. Download and preserve the period's GSTR-2B

Download the statement for the correct tax period and save the original file in the return working-paper folder. Record the period and download date. If a later review uses a refreshed or amended source file, preserve that version too so the reviewer can see which data was used.

GSTR-2B can include supplier-reported documents and certain import information received through the ICEGATE system. Do not assume every record will be found in the same section of the statement. Review the relevant tables and document types.

3. Match using document details, not just the amount

A practical match should consider supplier GSTIN, document type, invoice number, invoice date and taxable/tax amounts. Amount-only matching is risky because two unrelated invoices can have the same value. Normalise spacing and common punctuation in a helper field if needed, but keep the original number visible for verification.

For amended invoices, credit notes and debit notes, compare the original document with the amendment trail. A changed value may be a valid amendment rather than a new missing invoice.

4. Put every difference into an exception category

  • In books, not in GSTR-2B: check whether the supplier has filed the document in the relevant period, whether the GSTIN or invoice number was entered incorrectly, or whether the document belongs to another period.
  • In GSTR-2B, not in books: confirm that the purchase belongs to the business and the correct period. Check for duplicate booking, an unrecorded invoice or a supplier-side error.
  • Value or tax difference: compare the invoice, credit/debit note, tax rate, place of supply and any amendment.
  • Possible duplicate: check the document number, supplier GSTIN and accounting entry before deciding whether the credit has already been recorded or claimed.
  • Reverse charge or import: review these separately under the applicable process rather than treating them as an ordinary domestic supplier invoice.

5. Review ITC eligibility separately

A document appearing in GSTR-2B is not, by itself, a complete eligibility decision. Check the applicable provisions of the CGST Act and Rules, the nature and business use of the supply, required documents, receipt of goods or services, time limits and blocked-credit provisions. The GST Portal also cautions that other ineligible-credit situations may exist even when they are not shown as unavailable in the statement.

Where reverse charge applies, identify the liability and payment requirement separately. Do not treat a matching exercise as proof that all tax or credit conditions have been met.

6. Tie the review to the return working papers

Prepare a summary that shows opening differences, matched documents, additions, reversals, amendments and unresolved exceptions. Tie the reviewed figures to the relevant GSTR-3B working tables. Check that the same document has not been considered twice and that reversals required under the law have been evaluated.

A simple Excel control layout

Use columns such as Supplier GSTIN | Document No. | Document Date | Books Taxable Value | 2B Taxable Value | Books Tax | 2B Tax | Match Status | ITC Review | Action Owner | Evidence / Remarks. Add filters for the match status and keep unresolved items visible until they are closed or formally carried forward.

Final review checklist

  • Correct tax period and original GSTR-2B file retained
  • Purchase register complete and source invoice references preserved
  • Duplicate, amended and credit-note cases reviewed
  • Reverse-charge and import items reviewed separately
  • ITC eligibility assessed independently of the match result
  • GSTR-3B working figures tied to the reconciliation
  • Unresolved items assigned and supported with evidence
Key point

Reconciliation is a control process, not a target of zero differences. A well-documented exception is safer than an unsupported match or an automatic ITC claim.
